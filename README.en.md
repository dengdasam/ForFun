# for-fun · Guangxi Laibin Eco-Gardening Manuals

> A collection of planting manuals localized for **Guangxi Laibin climate** (annual avg 20.7°C / 331 frost-free days / 1360mm rainfall / acidic red soil).
> All are single-file HTML, light-background with dark-text (no white-text readability issues), openable directly in a browser.

---

## 📍 Shared Adaptation Background

**Laibin climate constants**
- Annual avg **20.7°C** ｜ Jan avg 10.9°C ｜ Jul avg 28.6°C ｜ extreme high 38.9°C
- **331 frost-free days** (≈ USDA Zone 9+) ｜ rainfall 1360mm (70% in Apr–Aug) ｜ 1580–1660h sunlight
- Soil: **acidic, heavy red soil**

**Red-soil iron rules (before planting)**
1. Amend acidity (agricultural lime)
2. Inoculate with rhizobium
3. Green-manure the soil (till under)
4. Wait **2–3 weeks** after tilling before transplanting
5. Garden trio: **rain-shelter + shade net + raised beds** (shade net mandatory at 35°C+ in Jul–Aug)
6. Black-soldier-fly frass is alkaline & calcium-rich — supplements red-soil deficiencies, prevents tomato blossom-end rot
